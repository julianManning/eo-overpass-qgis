about=Predicts when Landsat 8/9 and Sentinel-2A/B/C will image a region of interest. SGP4 orbits from CelesTrak TLEs; the instrument swath (MSI 290 km, OLI 185 km) is built with WGS84 geodesics and intersected with the ROI in an ROI-centred equal-area projection, giving ROI coverage %, entry/exit and scene-centre times, view angle and sun elevation, reported in UTC and the ROI's local time (DST-aware). Sentinel-2 passes can be cross-checked against ESA's published acquisition plans. Exports a CSV report, a standalone live-tracker web page and printable PNGs (pass timeline, A4 field sheet, orbit globe). Includes a live tracker dock (positions, ground tracks, current swaths, night side). Processing algorithms work in the Graphical Modeler, batch mode and qgis_process. No account or API key needed; pure-Python dependencies (sgp4, geographiclib, tzdata) are bundled. Validated against delivered Landsat and Sentinel-2 acquisition times (~1 s with fresh TLEs). Companion notebook toolkit: https://github.com/julianManning/eo-overpass-predictor
